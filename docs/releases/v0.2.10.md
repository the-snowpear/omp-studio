## 项目菜单与会话

右键项目行或点击「⋯」，可以打开该项目的终端，也可以从侧栏移除项目。项目文件会保留在磁盘上。默认保留会话，重新打开原目录即可查看历史。若选择永久删除，会清理该项目的活动会话、归档会话和关联的托管产物。清理未完成时，项目会保留在列表中并显示结果。 [#68](https://github.com/the-snowpear/omp-studio/pull/68)

切换项目时，各个已打开的对话继续使用各自的工作区和会话历史。

## 本版修复

- 长模型名称和完整的 `provider/model` 可以换行。把鼠标移到思考图标上，可查看支持的档位和待探测状态。 [#69](https://github.com/the-snowpear/omp-studio/issues/69)
- Bash 工具卡片一收到本次调用的结果就结束加载。同一轮里的后续工具调用使用各自的状态。 [#70](https://github.com/the-snowpear/omp-studio/issues/70)
- SSH 恢复时更新连接状态。远端文件下载完成后才替换目标文件，中断时保留已有副本。 [SSH 工作区](https://github.com/the-snowpear/omp-studio/blob/v0.2.10/docs/ssh-workspaces.md)
- Runtime 启动或重连时暂时收起输入预测。Windows 安装向导会在获取完整的安装路径后显示完成页。

## English summary

- Right-click a project row or open its menu to open a terminal for that project or remove it from the sidebar. Sessions are kept by default. You can also choose to permanently delete active and archived sessions and their managed artifacts. Project files stay on disk. [#68](https://github.com/the-snowpear/omp-studio/pull/68)
- Model names and full selectors wrap in narrow pickers. The thinking badge shows available levels on hover. Each Bash tool card stops spinning as soon as its own result arrives. [#69](https://github.com/the-snowpear/omp-studio/issues/69) · [#70](https://github.com/the-snowpear/omp-studio/issues/70)
